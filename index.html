WHAT WE DO

A full security solution, not just a guard on a gate.

From manned guarding to mobile patrols and alarm response, every service is built around your site's actual risk — not a one-size-fits-all contract.

01 · GUARDING

Manned Guarding & Access Control

Screened, vetted and uniformed officers controlling entry, egress and visitor access at your premises around the clock.

02 · PATROLS

Mobile Patrols

Scheduled and randomised patrol routes on foot, by vehicle, or with dogs — keeping remote corners of your site covered day and night.

03 · RESPONSE

Alarm & Armed Response

Linked to our control room, uniformed response officers are dispatched the moment an alarm is triggered, often reaching site before police.

04 · SURVEILLANCE

CCTV & Electronic Surveillance

On-site camera installation with looped recording and live monitoring, giving full-site visibility beyond what a single guard can see.

05 · PROTECTION

Close Protection

Trained personal protection officers for individuals, executives and high-profile events requiring a discreet or visible presence.

06 · INVESTIGATIONS

Investigations & Venue Security

Discreet, professional investigation support alongside crowd control and venue security for events of any size.

WHY BSS PROTECTION

Local knowledge, disciplined standards.

Founded in 2013 and based in Port Shepstone, we've grown by protecting mining sites, government offices, construction projects and retail premises across the UGU district — building the kind of local knowledge and on-site training that keeps our officers a step ahead.

Every officer works under a written code of ethics aligned to the Private Security Industry Regulation Act, and every site is backed by a control room in constant contact with personnel on the ground.
